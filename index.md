StrikeCam turns your iPhone into a golf launch monitor. Put the phone about a metre from the ball, place the ball in the green box on screen, and hit. For every shot you get carry distance (estimated), ball speed and launch angle — no extra hardware, no account.

## Support

Questions, problems or ideas: **rpetersson@gmail.com**

**Getting good readings**

- Use good light: bright daylight or a strong lamp on the ball. In dim light the shutter has to stay open longer and the ball blurs.
- Hold the phone in landscape, level, about a metre from the ball. The app tells you if it should move.
- Place the ball inside the green box and wait for **Ready — hit!**
- Choose the club you're hitting with the club button: it sets the spin used for the carry estimate and the size of the box.

**About the numbers.** Ball speed and launch angle are measured from the video. Carry and club speed are estimates based on the club you choose, standard air and no wind. StrikeCam doesn't measure spin or left/right direction.
