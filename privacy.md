_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

StrikeCam is a golf launch monitor that runs entirely on your iPhone.

## What StrikeCam uses

- **Camera** — to film your shot at high frame rate and measure ball speed and launch angle.
- **Microphone** — to hear the sound of club on ball, so a shot is only counted when it really happened and timed precisely. Sound is analysed as it arrives and is never recorded or stored.
- **Motion sensors** — to tell whether the phone is level.

## What StrikeCam keeps

Your shots (the numbers, a short slow-motion clip and a still image of each shot) and your settings are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. You can delete any shot in the app, and deleting the app removes everything.

## What StrikeCam shares

Nothing. StrikeCam has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet, and no video, sound, shots or personal information leave your device.

## Children

StrikeCam does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
